
# Privacy Policy for Browser Bible

## Last Updated: October 12, 2026
Thank you for choosing Browser Bible. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our Google Chrome extension interacts with data.

### 1. Data Collection and Usage

Browser Bible does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, user data, or browsing history.
• We do not track what you search for or what passages you read.
• We do not use cookies, analytics tools, or third-party tracking scripts.
• Your extension preferences (if any) are stored locally on your device and are never sent to us.

### 2. Network Permissions & Third-Party APIs

Our extension requests network access (host_permissions) exclusively for:
• https://bible-api.com/*
This permission is strictly used to fetch the Bible verses and text you request from the public, open-source Bible API. We do not transmit any identifying user data to this API. Please note that your interactions with the Bible API are subject to their own network privacy standards, but no personal information from your browser is passed along by Browser Bible.

### 3. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the "Last Updated" date at the top will be revised accordingly.

### 4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store support tab.
